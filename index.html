Who I Am

About Me

I am a third-year Mining Engineering student at The Copperbelt University in Zambia, passionate about combining traditional engineering with modern technology.

My studies in mining engineering give me a strong foundation in engineering principles, technical analysis and problem solving. Beyond the classroom, I actively explore software development, artificial intelligence, database systems, automation and data analysis — building practical tools that solve real operational problems.

I believe the future of mining lies in intelligent, data-driven operations. That is why I work at the intersection of engineering and technology — designing databases, developing applications and automating processes that make engineering work faster, safer and smarter.
